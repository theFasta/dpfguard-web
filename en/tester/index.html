DPF Guard

Instructions for testers

Last updated: 2 November 2026 (version 1.6.1+75)

Thank you for helping us! With the test version of DPF Guard you record what your car's engine computer says while you drive. From that data we find out where your engine keeps the particulate filter values (soot, regeneration, km), so that the app can show them.

In short: choose the engine, run the Full scan once until «Done!», then open the Probe on every drive and send us your drives from «Saved drives». Everything else runs by itself.

Before you start

  1. Update DPF Guard from the Play Store. Test versions sometimes show up a few hours later.
  2. Check that you have the right version: in DPF Guard tap the i icon at the top right (Info). It must say 1.6.1+75 or a higher number.

1. Choose the engine

Before connecting to the dongle, tap the icon at the top left and choose the engine we told you for your car.

If you use more than one car, change the engine every time you change car: it is the easiest mistake to make.

2. Connect and open the Probe

With the engine running, tap Search for devices and pick your dongle. Once connected, tap the Probe icon at the top right.

If your engine is not supported yet, the DPF screen stays empty or shows only a few values: that is normal, you are not doing anything wrong. The values come precisely from the drives you send us. (The picture next to this text comes from an engine that is already supported.)

3. The Full scan, once only

Inside the Probe tap the magnifying glass with lines icon, then Start. The scan tries every address of the engine computer: about 77 minutes in all. Read only, it changes nothing.

  • You can do it during a normal drive: no need to stand still.
  • No need to do it all at once: if you switch off the engine or leave the Probe, next time it carries on by itself from where it stopped.
  • While it runs, don't touch the other icons of the Probe.
  • When it says «Done!» it is no longer needed: send us that drive (step 5).

If we told you that the scan is already done for your car, skip this step.

4. On every drive

  1. Before leaving: connect to the dongle and open the Probe.
  2. Drive normally, without touching the phone: the Probe records by itself and saves the drive every minute and when you leave.
  3. If you notice that a regeneration has started (fan staying on, higher idle, burning smell) and you are stopped safely, you can tap Segna evento ("mark event", the button at the bottom right, still in Italian). It is not required: the drive is useful anyway.

5. Sending us the data

In the Probe tap Saved drives (from version 1.6.1+76 there is also the folder icon on the home screen: no need to connect to the dongle), then Send on the drive you want to send (or Send all). The file goes by email, WhatsApp or however you prefer.

No need to send every drive. We mostly need:

  • the drive in which the Full scan says «Done!»;
  • the drives in which a regeneration started;
  • the drives we ask you for by email.

Your data

The files contain no personal data: only the values read from the engine computer. Of the chassis number (VIN) the app keeps only the first 11 characters, which tell make, model and year but not which car it is. Details in the privacy policy.

Questions?

Write to support@dpfguard.it, even just to ask whether you are doing it right. Thank you!
